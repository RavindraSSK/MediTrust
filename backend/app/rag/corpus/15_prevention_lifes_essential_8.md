The American Heart Association's Life's Essential 8 summarises cardiovascular health across eight domains: diet, physical activity, nicotine exposure, sleep, body mass index, blood lipids, blood glucose and blood pressure. Higher cardiovascular health scores are associated with lower rates of cardiovascular disease and all-cause mortality across the life course.

For patients whose evaluation suggests low risk, guideline-based care focuses on confirming the absence of high-risk features, addressing modifiable risk factors such as blood pressure, lipids, glucose and smoking, and arranging appropriate follow-up rather than proceeding to further invasive testing.
