In the Cleveland Heart Disease data set the variable "ca" records the number of major coronary vessels, from 0 to 3, that showed narrowing on fluoroscopy at angiography. The outcome label used to train the model is defined as a diameter narrowing of 50 percent or more in at least one major vessel, so the vessel count is closely related to the outcome and is one of the most influential inputs.

Prognosis worsens with the anatomic extent of coronary artery disease. In the Coronary Artery Surgery Study registry, long-term survival of medically treated patients declined as the number of diseased vessels increased, and left main or proximal left anterior descending involvement carried additional risk. Multivessel disease, particularly in patients with diabetes or reduced left ventricular function, is the setting in which coronary revascularisation offers the clearest survival benefit according to the 2021 revascularisation guideline.
